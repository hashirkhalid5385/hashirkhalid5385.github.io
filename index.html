Every project starts with the outcome, not the tool. What needs to be true at the end? Who is it for, and what should they do, feel, or achieve?

Choose the right stack

I select tools by fit, not by trend. The right AI models, platforms, and workflows for the specific problem, budget, and timeline at hand.
